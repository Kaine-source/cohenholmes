About

Morpeth-based.
Outcome-obsessed.

Cohen & Holmes is an independent Microsoft 365 and Digital Workplace consultancy based in Morpeth, Northumberland. We specialise in the intersection of security, identity and AI — helping mid-market and enterprise organisations get more from their M365 investment without compromising on governance.

Our approach is direct and technical. We assess what's actually in your tenant, identify what matters, and deliver recommendations you can act on. No generic frameworks, no unnecessary complexity — just clear thinking and deep M365 expertise.

We hold certifications across the Microsoft security and AI stack and work alongside internal IT teams, Microsoft partners and managed service providers across the UK.
